# Withdrawing a scheduled cancellation

A live billing Owner or Manager can withdraw a scheduled cancellation from merchant Billing → Access status until the moment it takes effect. The confirmation names the original end, which does not change, and explains that the usual grace period applies again. The withdrawal changes no term dates, paid facts, price, anchor, policy or covered shops. It moves no money.

A withdrawal is the next immutable revision in `scheduled_subscription_changes`. Odd revisions schedule and even revisions withdraw the schedule just before them. The withdrawal row keeps the original `effective_at` and links to the withdrawn change. It gets its own actor-bound receipt in `subscription_cancellation_receipts`. Nothing is updated or deleted. Shop-scoped readers see a withdrawal through the withdrawn change's frozen shops. After a withdrawal, the term status omits `cancellation` and shows the original `graceEndsAt`. The term can take coverage again, and a new cancellation can be scheduled at the next odd revision.

The signed billing relay provides `POST …/cancellation/withdrawal` and original-actor receipt lookup at `GET …/cancellation/withdrawal/operations/{operationKey}`. The request carries `operationKey`, the active cancellation's `currentVersion`, `changeId`, `acceptedEffectiveAt` and `confirmed`. Authorization and the account fence come first. A stored receipt for the key then replays byte-for-byte, even after the boundary, and appends and publishes nothing. Otherwise the lifecycle clock is read inside the fence. A request at or after `effectiveAt` answers 409 `Billing.CancellationEffective`. A stale version, change or end answers 409 `Billing.CancellationConflict`. The database also refuses a withdrawal recorded at or after its effective instant. The withdrawal, receipt and republished shop entitlement snapshots commit in one transaction. The snapshots carry the original access end and leave the account restriction head untouched, so a withdrawal or retry cannot release a suspension.

The merchant browser freezes the withdrawal command per actor, account and term before sending. Check looks up the same key and Retry resends the identical command. A refused send is final and discards the command. Any other failure keeps it. After the receipt arrives, the page refreshes the status and explains that covered shops receive the change through the existing synchronization status. Provider Access status stops showing the cancellation for that term. It needs no new screen.

Apply licensing migration `20261116220000_AddSubscriptionCancellationWithdrawals` after `20261116210000_AddScheduledSubscriptionCancellations`, then upgrade every licensing API replica before accepting a withdrawal. Earlier binaries cannot read a term with more than one change row. Deploy the merchant API relay and both web consumers after that. The migration can be rolled back only while no withdrawal or later revision exists. After one does, preserve the evidence and keep withdrawal-aware readers in service. Phase 2 remains observe-only.

For local verification, run `P0216Tests` against real PostgreSQL and build both web consumers. Then run `P0216BrowserTests` with `P0216_RUN_BROWSER=1` and `P0216_EVIDENCE_DIR` set to your local evidence directory. The browser run uses only local fixture prices, actors, subscriptions and transport.
