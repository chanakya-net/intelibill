# Till sessions and day close design

Date: 2026-11-08. Base: `fad87708` on `codex/till-sessions-day-close`.

This implements the supplied “Till sessions and day close Implementation Plan” (market-readiness item 33). It preserves accountable opening, all cash inflows/outflows, separate recorded tender totals, source-linked immutable movements, counted closing, cashier responsibility, variance, concurrency safety, and visible late offline reconciliation. Web/API only.

## Product decisions

- One open session per named physical till, with one named active shop member as cashier. Owner/Manager creates tills. Cashiers open and operate their own sessions; Owner/Manager may act for another cashier only with a persisted override reason. Existing source permissions still apply: a till does not grant Staff permission to issue refunds or supplier payments.
- Handover closes the old session and opens a new one with a fresh counted float; no reassignment of a live drawer. A cashier may select a till explicitly; never infer a session from “first open till.”
- An enabled shop requires an explicit open session for every new in-scope transaction, including non-cash and credit-only sales so totals and cashier attribution remain complete. Historical corrections with explicitly no money movement are the exception; they still retain their own source audit and explicit settlement disposition.
- `TillSessions:Enabled=false`, `AllowedShopIds=[]`, `MixedTenderEnabled=false` by default. Both global enablement and exact allowlisting are required. Mixed tender remains unavailable even if a configuration is accidentally set true: reject startup or reject the unsupported capability, never silently enable it. Disabled shops retain existing request behavior and legacy hashes when new fields are absent. A supplied till request may never silently fall back to an untracked posting when the gate is off. Existing committed operation replay and reports remain available after disablement.
- Sessions accept trading only on their opening shop-local business date. Normal close is same-day. An overdue session blocks further trading and opening another session on that till. **Coordinator ruling:** Owner/Manager may explicitly finalize an overdue session with a reason, preserving its original business date and actual late count/close timestamp. This is a visibly overdue count, not permission for multi-day trading. Fence the original period; a closed month requires reopening, never redating.
- Counted cash and nonzero variance reason are mandatory. A Staff close with a variance is frozen immediately and marked awaiting manager review; Owner/Manager acknowledges it in an append-only review record with a reason. The acknowledgement never changes counted cash, expected cash, variance or cashier.
- “Recorded UPI/card/bank transfer/cheque” describes cashier evidence, not independently verified settlement. No gateway collection, automated refunds, bank reconciliation, shared live drawers, partial session close, reopening a closed session or mobile workflow.

## Existing seams and gaps

`RecordSaleCommandHandler` owns its transaction and sale idempotency. `IFinancialOperationExecutor` owns keyed mutation transactions and stores responses; authorization precedes replay. Customer/supplier payments use `PaymentTransactionRunner`; their receipt tender may be `UnknownRecordedMethod`. Supplier payments also create an Expense, so counting both records would double the cash outflow.

`SaleReturnMutationCoordinator` fences the period before customer/sale locks and owns its transaction. Return `Refund` is a general destination, **not cash evidence**. `Expense` has no payment tender. `ReversePaymentCommandHandler` reverses bookkeeping and allocations but does not prove money returned. `CorrectExpenseCommandHandler` voids/replaces an expense; that is not proof of cash recovered or paid again. Cancellation already records operator-confirmed cash refund. These paths need explicit contracts, not a universal “invert amount” hook.

Offline sync persists an immutable submission envelope and uses `OfflineSalePoster` for both sync and historical replay. The posting-period fence lives inside the poster; held cases are saved after rollback. Durable web examples are checkout intents and `SaleCancellationDraftService` (owner keys, atomic revision claims, unknown status and confirmation tombstones).

## Data and contracts

All new tables carry `ShopId`, tenant predicates and enforced RLS following existing migration/restricted-role fixtures. Composite tenant foreign keys prevent cross-shop references. Amounts are decimal INR with two fractional digits; reject excess precision, negatives where unsigned, undefined enums and invalid references before persistence. Database checks/unique constraints defend the invariants too.

`Till`: Id, ShopId, normalized unique Name, CreatedBy/At. No deletion of used tills.

`TillSession`: Id, ShopId, TillId, CashierId, frozen TillName/CashierName, OpenedBy/At, BusinessDate, BusinessTimeZoneId, OpeningFloat, State(Open/Closed), LastMovementSequence, `xmin` Version. Partial unique index `(ShopId,TillId) WHERE state=Open`. Every appended movement increments the session sequence and changes its row version. A close rejects an old expected version, including one made stale by a sale.

`TillMovement`: Id, ShopId, SessionId, monotonic session Sequence, SourceType, SourceId, Purpose, LineNumber, Method, SignedAmount, CashTendered/Change where supplied, ExternalReference, BankAccountId, OccurredAt (source event), RecordedAt, ActorUserId, OperationKey, Reason/OverrideReason, optional ReversesMovementId. Unique `(ShopId,SourceType,SourceId,Purpose,LineNumber)` globally across sessions; unique `(ShopId,SessionId,Sequence)`. Never update/delete. Sources are server-owned identifiers, never arbitrary client-selected “linked sale” cash-in entries.

`TillClose`: one row per session, frozen OpeningFloat, LastMovementSequence, MovementCount, signed totals by method and purpose, ExpectedCash, CountedCash, Variance, ClosedBy/At, reason, overdue flag, original BusinessDate, cashier/till labels. Read this snapshot directly; never reconstruct a historical close by joining mutable source records. A separate `TillCloseReview` freezes reviewer/time/reason and references the close.

`TillLatePosting`: unique per offline submission/sale, optional historical SessionId and CloseId, CaseId, source issue date/time, recorded time, attributed cashier, actual tender lines/evidence status, manager reason. This is a supplemental reconciliation disclosure with `IncludedInCount=false`, never a current drawer movement. It may explain a historical variance but never relabels the original count as reconciled or adjusts its totals.

Shared domain value `TenderLine(ReceiptTenderMethod Method, decimal AppliedAmount, decimal? CashTendered, decimal? Change, string? ExternalReference, Guid? BankAccountId)`. For paid sales/payment/refund amounts, exactly one external line when amount > 0; none when amount=0. Sum equals paid/payout amount. Cash pair is either both absent (only net cash was recorded) or both present with tendered ≥ applied and `tendered - change == applied`; non-cash disallows either field. Never synthesize cash tendered/change from applied amount. Reject `UnknownRecordedMethod` for new enabled postings. Map PaymentMethod explicitly: Cash1→Cash2, UPI2→Upi3, Card3→Card4; Credit4 is not an external tender. Preserve legacy PaymentMethod/PaidAmount and invoice arithmetic/document snapshots. Store credit issued/redeemed and receivable due/reduction are separate non-drawer settlement totals, not external tender lines.

Shared application request `TillPostingContext(Guid SessionId, string? OverrideReason)`; it is frozen into every source operation hash and durable request. No client shop/actor values are trusted.

For bookkeeping reversals/corrections use `TillSettlementEvidence(Disposition, TenderLine? Tender, string Reason)`, with Disposition `NoPhysicalMovement`, `MoneyReceived`, `MoneyPaid`. Enabled requests must state the disposition. NoPhysicalMovement appends no cash and may not contain a tender. Other dispositions require a till context and a concrete tender; enforce source direction and amount rules below. Persist evidence with the source reversal/correction, including no-movement evidence. Never treat an original method as proof of reversal tender.

## Source integration matrix

| Source | Drawer effect and evidence | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| Online sale | +external paid line; store-credit redemption separately | Session required even all-due; line sum = PaidAmount; cash ₹500 tendered, ₹200 change, ₹300 applied adds ₹300. Include new fields in idempotency hash without changing legacy absent-field hashes. |
| Customer payment receipt | +receipt amount using recorded method | Enabled requires explicit known method, operation key and till; source is receipt, allocations have no cash effect. |
| Supplier payment receipt | −receipt amount using recorded method | One movement only; linked supplier Expense is provenance, never another outflow. |
| Independent expense | −explicit tender amount equal to expense amount | Enabled requires known tender and operation key, session date = expense date = today. Persist expense settlement evidence. Historical bookkeeping entry requires explicit NoPhysicalMovement rather than unknown cash. |
| Expense correction | Only explicitly declared new receipt/payment | NoPhysicalMovement leaves drawer unchanged; money paid/received is an explicit signed adjustment equal to the exact signed corrected-vs-original difference. Same-amount metadata corrections have no cash. Never erase old movement. Prevent SupplierSettlement edits through this route. |
| Sale return | −actual external refund; +store credit issued separately | Refund destination requires exact payout tender and operator confirmation. Receivable reduction has no cash. Store credit issuance is not a refund tender. Key whole return atomically in enabled flow. |
| Return void | +explicit recovered refund, or no physical movement | Never auto-invert generic Refund. Recovery at most original actual payout, with exact amount for first-release whole recovery; explicit no-recovery gives no cash. Existing stock/accounting void constraints remain. Current session receives confirmed recovered money, not original session. |
| Sale cancellation | −existing exact operator-confirmed cash refund | Current session/till before customer/sale locks, same transaction, source cancellation id. Existing cancellation capability and eligibility remain mandatory. |
| Customer payment reversal | −explicit returned amount, or no physical movement | Whole receipt amount for actual return; original payment unchanged. |
| Supplier payment reversal | +explicit recovered amount, or no physical movement | Whole receipt amount; linked expense void contributes no second movement. |
| Manual cash-in/out | Signed amount, cashier, mandatory reason | New server-owned source/idempotent operation, no arbitrary financial source-link field. These are transfers, not income/expenses. |
| Supplier return accepted credit / purchase receipt reversal | None | Payable adjustments/stock shipments are not confirmed cash refunds. |
| Late offline sale | Supplemental record after explicit review | No movement added to closed or current session; original invoice and close immutable. |

## Serialization and services

Lock order for all affected paths: operation receipt → reconciliation case (when applicable) → sorted posting-period fences → till/session → customer/supplier → sale/receipt → credit notes → batches. Read-only preflight may happen earlier, but locked mutable state must be reloaded. `FOR NO KEY UPDATE`/bounded five-second budget, restore prior lock timeout, map contention to `Till.Busy`; use existing period errors. Close takes the same period/session locks as posting and computes totals only after lock acquisition.

`ITillPostingService.AcquireAsync(shopId, actorId, TillPostingContext?, sourceBusinessDate, ct)` returns either a transaction-bound session or disabled/no-context result; it checks gate, live membership, cashier/override, date and open state. It **does not acquire the period fence**: the caller must already have all needed period fences. `AppendAsync(lockedSession, TillSourcePosting, ct)` stages movements and advances the sequence; no transaction/save/commit inside. Both use the source transaction/unit of work. Till locking must be inserted into the return coordinator before its sale lock, not inside the callback after sale locks; add a pre-business-lock hook/overload and an existing-transaction execution path for keyed returns without nested transactions.

Till lifecycle commands use `IFinancialOperationExecutor`, mandatory OperationKey and ExpectedVersion for movement/close/review. Opening serializes the physical Till row then checks partial uniqueness. Authorize actor and target before replay. Source posting authorization must also precede replay, but committed response replay must not fail because its original session is now closed or rollout disabled. Distinguish replay authorization from validation of a new posting.

## API and UI

`GET /api/tills/capabilities` → `{enabled,mixedTenderEnabled:false,businessDate}`. `GET/POST /api/tills` list/create physical tills. `GET /api/tills/sessions?state=&businessDate=` paged visible sessions; Staff only their own. `GET /api/tills/sessions/{id}` returns session, current totals, paged source movements, frozen close/review and separately labeled late postings. Owner/Manager may inspect all in their shop.

`POST /api/tills` takes `{name,operationKey}` and returns the saved till. `POST /api/tills/{id}/sessions` `{cashierId,openingFloat,operationKey,overrideReason?}`. `POST /api/tills/sessions/{id}/cash-movements` `{direction,amount,reason,expectedVersion,operationKey,overrideReason?}`. `POST .../close` `{countedCash,reason?,expectedVersion,operationKey,overrideReason?,finalizeOverdue:false}`. `POST .../review` `{reason,expectedVersion,operationKey}`. Session mutations return saved `TillSessionDto` with fresh version and snapshot. Typed lookup `/api/tills/operations/{create|open|cash-movement|close|review}/{key}` supports Staff's own receipt only and managers' shop receipts; avoid widening generic financial lookup permissions. Register expense-record, expense-correction, sale-return and sale-return-void in the existing financial-operation lookup for Owner/Manager durable recovery.

`/tills` shows capability, till/session selection, opening float, named cashier, current expected cash and separate recorded tender/credit totals, source links, cash in/out, counted close, variance reason/review and history. `/tills/sessions/:id/print` prints saved close reference, original date, actual close time, cashier, opening/expected/counted/variance, included boundary, recorded tender totals and separate supplemental late disclosures. Show overdue counts explicitly.

Reusable till selection/evidence controls integrate checkout, customer/supplier payment, expense/correction, return/void, cancellation and payment reversal. They freeze session id and evidence before sending and never rewrite a pending payload after switching tills. Every new keyed mutation uses an IndexedDB durable action draft keyed by user/shop/action/target with revision CAS, frozen request/key, unknown status and confirmation proof. Unknown outcomes (including 404 lookup, stale scope, timeout, reload/tab race) retain the same key; no edit/discard/new request until conclusively settled. Failure to persist blocks HTTP. Existing source flows use existing durable infrastructure when it has those semantics. Scope epoch guards and request ownership remain mandatory. All supported locales get keys.

## Offline handling

New offline invoices freeze selected session id, its opening business date/cashier and tender lines in the existing queue/snapshot protocol. Offline start requires previously verified enabled capability and an own open same-day session; it cannot open/close sessions offline. Preserve legacy version/hash compatibility; lack of till fields on legacy queued invoices is not “cash” or automatic assignment.

At sync, after original-date period fence and before business locks, lock referenced session. If still open on its original day and invoice issue time is within its interval with verified cashier, post source + till movement atomically. Otherwise hold as new `TillReview`, retaining issue date, invoice number and envelope. Resending a held case never bypasses it even after opening another session. A close may precede unsynced invoices; UI warns about locally pending invoices, but server safety does not depend on the client knowing every device.

Owner/Manager action `ReconcileTillSale` validates frozen envelope and actual tender evidence, original interval/cashier if claimed, and original posting period. It posts the unchanged sale through the common poster in an explicit reviewed mode and writes one `TillLatePosting` with case/sale/close links, **without appending drawer cash**. For legacy missing till/tender context, record manager-supplied evidence separately on the reconciliation record, never mutate the envelope or invent a historical session; unknown evidence remains visibly unknown and cannot be labeled cash. New incomplete enabled invoices are refused/reviewed rather than guessed. Existing historical-lease replay cannot bypass this requirement; both paths converge on poster enforcement. Any later money physically put into a drawer is an independently declared manual transfer, not an automatic repeat of this sale.

A verified-issuer `TillReview` envelope with complete frozen Till, TenderLines, TillBusinessDate and TillCashierId may use Owner/Manager `RetryHistoricalLease` through the same enforcing poster. This is a bounded ordinary retry: it posts to the original drawer only if the gate is enabled and the original period, session, issue interval, cashier, tender and existing invoice policies all pass. A disabled-first-sync invoice can therefore enter its original still-open same-day count after re-enable. This ordinary TillReview retry does not mark customer credit as reviewed: normal offline customer-credit variance filing remains active. Missing or partial frozen context offers only explicit reconciliation, so disabling the gate cannot send a legacy TillReview into untracked legacy posting. Empty tender lines count as present for zero-paid invoices. Closed, overdue, missing or inconsistent context still refuses ordinary retry; device resends remain held until an explicit resolution. `ReconcileTillSale` refuses supplemental posting for a valid current open session (`Till.ReviewNotRequired`), so review is never an alternative way to omit includable drawer cash.

Separately supplied `Recorded` legacy tender evidence must equal the original `PaidAmount` and must match an unambiguous original external method: Cash→Cash, UPI→Upi, Card→Card. A wholly legacy invoice recorded as `Credit` with positive `PaidAmount` does not identify its external tender; it may remain `Unknown` with no tender, or an Owner/Manager may supply a separately reasoned actual known single tender for exactly that paid amount. Preserve its original Credit method and envelope in both cases. This narrow historical ambiguity rule does not relax new-field invoices: those still require valid new-sale method conservation and exact equality to frozen tender lines.

## Verification and rollout

Acceptance fixture: float ₹1000 + cash sale ₹500 + collection ₹200 − confirmed refund ₹100 − expense ₹50 − cash-out ₹300 = expected ₹1250; counted ₹1240 gives −₹10. UPI ₹700 changes only recorded UPI. Also cover store-credit/due separation, ₹500/₹200 change, source replay, supplier double-counting, unsupported mixed tender, absent/unknown tender, forbidden cashier/tenant, override reason, same-day/overdue behavior, late immutable close, durable unknown requests and every reversal disposition.

Use real independent PostgreSQL contexts with barriers for sale-vs-close, payment-vs-close, double-close/open, period-close-vs-till and period-close-vs-payment. Assert coherent commits/no late movement/no deadlock; do not simulate serialization with an in-memory repository. Restricted-role RLS cases cover all new tables and source ids.

Migration before all API/readers/web, then explicit pilot allowlist only after opening count. No historical session assignment or tender backfill. Mixed-version old writers are unsafe for enabled shops; enable only after all writers enforce till context. Disable rollout to stop new till work, preserve replay/read/report and immutable facts; reconcile open sessions before disabling operationally. Do not deploy pre-feature binaries or run destructive Down after any posting. No deployment or pilot enablement is authorized by this code task.

Baseline observed by coordinator: backend unit 419 Domain +1764 Application +583 API pass; frontend 266 files/2596 tests plus 59 script tests pass.
