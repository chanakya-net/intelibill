Use the repository's PostgreSQL/Testcontainers fixtures and both shipping web builds. The fixtures provision separate licensing and merchant databases with their own migrations and runtime roles. Status reads, consent, sales, snapshots and acknowledgements use the normal HTTP endpoints. The focused integration tests control delivery timing and lifecycle time. The Chromium tests run the shipping merchant and provider apps against local HTTP hosts.

## Local demo and regression evidence

- Create an independent account in merchant Billing, invite and revoke a billing manager, then accept an explicit ownership transfer. In a different shop-owner session, grant and accept consent. Check the payer's lack of shop access, the unchanged merchant roles, the ownership race and the last-slot race using the PostgreSQL cases above.
- In provider Catalogue, publish a labeled local plan successor and retire its predecessor. Recover a historical agreement with its original version, price and capacity. Compare independent shop dates against the shared coverage date.
- Open trial terms as the live shop owner and accept once. In the provider view, preview and confirm a reasoned extension or complimentary grant with recent MFA. Compare the shown zoned deadline, basis and capacities. Use the fixed clock for exact end and eligible paid-grace cases. Schedule a cancellation, withdraw before the effective instant and check refusal at the effective instant.
- Delay snapshot delivery, then deliver while holding the acknowledgement. At both status APIs compare the saved versus last confirmed revisions. After the signed acknowledgement, compare them again. The P0214 browser drill also surfaces NeedsReview and recovery in both consumers. Inspect browser privacy and cache-control assertions for staff, billing-only and unrelated scopes.
- In `P0217Tests.ExistingShopTradesBeforeTrialAndWhileProjectionIsPendingOrApplied`, one stock batch supports four persisted cash sales. One precedes billing enrolment, the others run while queued, awaiting acknowledgement and applied. Replay the original sale key and check four v2 receipts, four outbound movements and the remaining unit. Check the licensing trial/coverage facts and the staff refusal.
- In `CrashedAndReorderedDeliveryCannotRefreshStaleEvidenceOrBlockObserveOnlySales`, deliver the newer trial snapshot first. Discard the HTTP response to the older affiliation after its PostgreSQL commit. Read the retained Superseded receipt, retry and compare receipt and upstream verification timestamps. Read both merchant heads at the 48-hour boundary ±1 μs. A completed sale succeeds while the diagnostic is Stale and the source still shows Active/Applied. The source verification scan then queues and confirms refreshed revisions without changing the deadline or the unrelated account.
- In `ExactTrialEndCannotBeRevivedByAnOlderSnapshot`, the fixed lifecycle clock evaluates Active, Expired and Expired at the end ±1 μs. A retained Active receipt can replay without replacing the Expired head. The original trial operation replays, a fresh operation answers AlreadyUsed, and a sale still commits. The merchant sale business time is independent of the licensing fixture clock.

## P03 contracts

| Contract | Version and operational meaning |
| --- | --- |
| [Time vectors](../../contracts/licensing/time-v1.json) | Version 1. `calendar-month-year/1`, `subscription-access/1`, `access-grace/1`, `renewal/1`. Use Asia/Kolkata calendar anchors with UTC microsecond instants and half-open intervals. Eligible paid renewal grace lasts seven elapsed days. |
| [Subscription status](../../contracts/licensing/subscription-status-v1.json) | Schema 1 with additive synchronization and cancellation fields. A running trial is `state=Active`, `term.basis=Trial`. Read the exact deadline and capacity from the term. Scheduled, EvidenceIncomplete and NeedsReview are wire states. |
| [Entitlement delivery](../../contracts/licensing/entitlement-snapshot-v1.json) | Schema 1, `entitlement-projection/1`, 8,192-byte limit, independent account and shop revisions, 48-hour freshness. Signed POSTs use `/internal/licensing/v1/entitlements`, `/internal/licensing/v1/merchant-events/acknowledgement` and `/internal/licensing/v1/merchant-events/reconciliation`. The snapshot response is a transport receipt. Source confirmation requires the separate authenticated acknowledgement. |
| [Trial](../../contracts/licensing/shop-trial-v1.json) and [complimentary grant](../../contracts/licensing/complimentary-grant-v1.json) | V1 acceptance binds the published policy ID, revision and terms version. Standard trials last exactly fourteen days with no charge or grace. Exceptions use `trial-extension/1` or `complimentary-access/1` and carry separate immutable facts without paid revenue. |
| [Cancellation and withdrawal](../../contracts/licensing/subscription-cancellation-v1.json) | Contract version 1, `term-boundary/1`. Scheduled revisions are odd. Open revisions are even, starting at zero. Withdrawal is allowed strictly before effect and preserves the purchased term and grace anchor. |

- **Pending** covers Queued, Retrying and AwaitingAcknowledgement. Use the saved revisions to describe the workflow and retain the previous confirmed revisions and time. A pending increase remains pending in the purchasing flow even if the snapshot request returned HTTP 200.
- **Applied** requires a validated signed acknowledgement confirming both saved revisions. Trial-operation Applied and affiliation Applied refer to their own writes. Each can precede entitlement synchronization.
- **NeedsReview** covers dead letter or an acknowledged review disposition and retains previous confirmation. Transport disabled yields NotAvailable. Stale is a merchant projection diagnostic independent of synchronization and source lifecycle. Replay keeps upstream verification times. Observe-only fixtures keep `enforcement_activated_at` null.

Apply licensing and merchant entitlement migrations `20261116200000_EntitlementDelivery` and `20261116200000_AddShopLicensingState`, both runtime-role grants, then the compatible APIs and relays before the synchronization UI readers. Apply licensing `20261116210000_AddScheduledSubscriptionCancellations` and `20261116220000_AddSubscriptionCancellationWithdrawals` before their writers. Keep evidence and migrated tables on application rollback. See the [licensing rollout guide](../../../docs/licensing-rollout.md).
